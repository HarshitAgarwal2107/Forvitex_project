# Company policies
## Home loan
Skyline Realty helps with home loan paperwork through partner banks. Banks decide eligibility, interest rate and approval, so we cannot promise them.
## Discounts
Prices on this call are starting prices. The assistant cannot offer or promise discounts. The sales manager can discuss current offers during the site visit.
## Site visits
Site visits are free, take about 45 minutes, and are hosted by a Skyline relationship manager. Family is welcome. Pickup can be requested within Bengaluru. A visit carries no obligation to buy.
## Cancellation and refund
A booking amount is refundable in full within 15 days of payment. After that the agreement terms apply.
## Maintenance
Maintenance is approximately 4 to 5 rupees per sq ft per month depending on the project.
## Legal documents
Title documents and approvals are shared by the relationship manager at the visit. The assistant does not give legal or tax advice.
## Resale and rental returns
Skyline does not guarantee appreciation or rental returns. The relationship manager can share recent local rental data.
## NRI buyers
NRI buyers are welcome. Video walkthroughs are available, and the relationship manager explains the paperwork.
