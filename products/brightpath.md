# Data Analyst Track
## Overview
Data Analyst Track runs 4 months with live classes on Saturday and Sunday, 10 AM to 1 PM. Suitable for beginners; no coding needed to start.
## Fees
Data Analyst Track costs 45,000 rupees. It can be paid in 3 equal monthly instalments with no interest.
## Curriculum
Data Analyst Track covers Excel, SQL, Python basics, Power BI and two portfolio projects.
# AI Engineer Track
## Overview
AI Engineer Track runs 6 months, Monday to Thursday, 7:30 PM to 9 PM online. Learners should already know basic Python.
## Fees
AI Engineer Track costs 85,000 rupees. It can be paid in 5 equal monthly instalments with no interest.
## Curriculum
AI Engineer Track covers machine learning, deep learning, LLM applications, deployment and a capstone project.
# Academy policies
## Job support
BrightPath offers resume reviews, mock interviews and a hiring-partner job board. It does not guarantee a job, placement or salary.
## Refund
Full refund if requested within 7 days of the first class. No refund after that.
## Certificate
A completion certificate is given with 75 percent attendance and all projects submitted. It is not a university degree.
## Missed classes
Every live class is recorded and available for 12 months.
## Counselling session
The counselling session is free, lasts 45 minutes, is held online with an advisor, and carries no obligation to enrol.
